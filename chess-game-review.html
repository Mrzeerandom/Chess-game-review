Runs a real chess engine (Stockfish, WebAssembly) live in your browser — fully offline, no account needed
This loads a ~7MB chess engine and analyzes every move in your browser — it can take anywhere from 30 seconds to a few minutes depending on game length, engine depth, and your device. Depth 10 is a good starting point; drop to 8 on an older phone if it's slow. Explanations for "Why?" are generated locally from verified engine facts — no network call, works offline once the engine's cached. This review is built around your moves: your own moves are scored normally against the engine's best, but your opponent's moves are only flagged when they created a chance you didn't take, or a threat you didn't answer, within your next 1-3 moves. The board orientation follows the colour you select above.
